Dataset Information

Data Source: Electric vehicle registration data across multiple states and counties

Time Range: February 2017 - February 2024

Coverage: 269 counties across 5+ states

Total Records: 12,573 data points

Vehicle Types: Passenger vehicles and Trucks

EV Categories: Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)

Forecasting Methodology

The forecasting model uses a time series approach with the following features:

  • Temporal Features: Months since start, seasonal patterns
  • Lag Features: Previous 1-3 months of EV totals
  • Rolling Statistics: 3-month moving averages
  • Growth Metrics: Percentage changes and growth slopes
  • Regional Encoding: County-specific patterns

The model provides confidence intervals to indicate prediction uncertainty.

Data Definitions

BEVs: Battery Electric Vehicles - fully electric vehicles
PHEVs: Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles - vehicles with both electric and gasoline power
EV Total: Combined count of BEVs and PHEVs
Percent Electric: Percentage of total vehicles that are electric
Vehicle Primary Use: Classification of vehicle usage (Passenger or Truck)

Limitations & Assumptions

  • Forecasts assume continuation of current policy and market conditions
  • External factors (economic changes, new regulations) not explicitly modeled
  • Model performance may vary by region based on historical data availability
  • Predictions are statistical estimates with inherent uncertainty
  • Data quality depends on reporting consistency across jurisdictions
